## Inventory Guidebooks

|---|---|---|

| IPCC 2019 Refinement to the 2006 Guidelines | 2019 | Updated EFs/methods where adopted (specify sector/category) |
| EMEP/EEA Air Pollutant Emission Inventory Guidebook | — | Cross-check / air pollutant EFs (if used) |
